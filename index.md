Football Quiz privacy policy

Effective date: 21 September 2026

Football Quiz is an offline football trivia game. This policy explains what the app does with information when you use it.

Information the app stores

The app lets you choose a nickname and favourite club. It also stores game progress such as quiz history, scores, XP, coins, streaks, ranks, achievements, and your settings. This information is stored only in Football Quiz's private storage on your device so the app can show your progress.

No account, advertising, or tracking

Football Quiz does not require an account and has no sign-in, social profile, ads, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or tracking. The app has no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, accounts, or cloud services. The Flutter shared_preferences package is used only to save the local game snapshot on the device; it does not send that snapshot to us.

No network or sharing

The question bank is included in the app. Football Quiz does not send your nickname, favourite club, answers, scores, device identifiers, or other personal information to a server, and it does not share them with other companies. The app has no cloud backup or synchronisation feature.

Deletion and retention

Your local game information remains on the device until you reset progress in Settings or uninstall the app. Uninstalling Football Quiz deletes the data held in the app's private storage, subject to the normal behaviour of your device and its backups.

Children

Football Quiz does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. It does not provide accounts or online communication.
